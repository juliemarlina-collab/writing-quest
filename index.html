How to Play & Controls

⌨️ Desktop & Camera Controls

  • W / A / S / D or Arrow Keys: Run & steer hero
  • Spacebar: Jump (bounce high on red mushrooms!)
  • Drag Mouse or C Key: Orbit 360° / Switch Selfie Camera
  • E Key / ✒️ WRITE: Open the anvil next to Quilla

🪞 See Yourself & Audio Features

  • 🎥 Cam Button: Switch between Close, Selfie Front, Action & Top views
  • 🪞 Mirror: Show or hide your Hero Mirror card
  • ✒️ Writing: Task on the left, your writing on the right. Write, then tap the sentences that show each note / PEEL part and tick the checklist. The model opens after that.
  • ⏱ Exam timing: optional 25-min (Task 1) or 50-min (Task 2) timer. The Mock Paper uses 75 min for both.
  • 🔤 A− / A+: Change the text size

🏆 Scoring & Star Rating Rules

✨ Quiz, 1st try: +100 pts • Writing: 100 per ⭐
✨ Correct, 2nd try: +50 pts
❌ Wrong twice: 0 pts (answer shown)
🪶 Golden Quill: +10 pts
🎁 Hidden Chest: +100 pts
🌀 Level Clear: +300 pts

📚 Texts & credits

All tasks, models and questions were written for this game, following the MUET 800/4 format (Task 1: email of 100+ words using all the notes; Task 2: essay of 250+ words; 75 minutes in total). Nothing is scored automatically: students check their own work, and the lecturer report includes their writing. 3D scenery: Kenney Nature Kit (CC0). 3D engine: Three.js.
